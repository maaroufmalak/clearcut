A few good answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscriptions, credits, or daily photo limits. Clearcut runs on your device and is open source.

Where do my photos go?

They stay in your browser. Clearcut does not upload your images or use analytics. Your browser downloads the AI model and site files from GitHub Pages; those requests do not contain your photos.

Why does the first photo take longer?

The first run downloads about 56 MB of AI and runtime files, then starts the model. Your browser may cache those files for later visits. Keep this tab open while it processes; speed depends on your device.

Will every cutout be perfect?

Fine hair, glass, shadows, and busy backgrounds can be challenging. For best results, choose a sharp photo with a clearly visible subject. Images larger than 4096 pixels on the longest side or 12 megapixels are resized to keep processing manageable. Modern desktop browsers work best; low-memory devices may struggle.
